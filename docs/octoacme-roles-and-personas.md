# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Leads

### Role Summary
Engineering Leads guide technical execution, team alignment, and delivery quality. They balance engineering feasibility with product priorities and support the project team in delivering robust, maintainable solutions.

### Responsibilities
- Own technical direction for the team or feature area
- Break down work into implementable engineering increments
- Coordinate with Product and PM on scope, sequencing, and trade-offs
- Support architecture decisions, code quality, and delivery standards
- Help unblock technical dependencies and escalate risks early

### Goals
- Deliver predictable, high-quality engineering execution
- Align technical decisions with product goals and delivery timelines
- Improve team throughput while maintaining maintainability and reliability

### Typical Communication
- Engineering planning and sprint reviews
- Technical dependency discussions with PM and Product
- Architecture decisions and delivery risk updates

---

## QA Leads / Test Managers

### Role Summary
QA Leads define the quality strategy, validation approach, and release readiness criteria for the project. They ensure that work meets acceptance criteria and customer expectations before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, coverage, and validation plans
- Review requirements and acceptance criteria for testability
- Coordinate test execution, defect triage, and release readiness reviews
- Partner with engineering and product on bug prioritization and quality gates
- Support continuous improvement of automated and manual validation processes

### Goals
- Reduce defects that reach production
- Improve confidence in release quality and customer experience
- Ensure quality checks are aligned with business risk and delivery goals

### Typical Communication
- Acceptance criteria reviews and QA sign-off meetings
- Defect triage with engineering leads and product stakeholders
- Release readiness and regression status updates

---

## Security Champions / Security Reviewers

### Role Summary
Security Champions help teams identify and mitigate security risks throughout the project lifecycle. They bring security considerations into design, implementation, and release decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Review features, integrations, and data flows for security risks
- Define security requirements and policy checks for the project
- Partner with engineering and PMs to assess risk and mitigation plans
- Coordinate escalation for sensitive findings or compliance concerns
- Support secure implementation patterns and remediation planning

### Goals
- Reduce risk of vulnerabilities, data exposure, and compliance issues
- Embed security into normal delivery workflows
- Improve confidence in release quality and risk management

### Typical Communication
- Security design reviews and architecture discussions
- Risk assessments with PM, Product, and engineering teams
- Incident and remediation updates when security concerns arise

---

## Technical Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Architects provide design guidance for system structure, integration patterns, and long-term maintainability. They help teams make sound technical decisions while keeping dependencies and scalability in view.

### Responsibilities
- Define major architecture patterns, integration approaches, and standards
- Evaluate trade-offs across performance, reliability, security, and maintainability
- Guide technical design reviews and cross-system dependencies
- Support engineering leads and developers during complex implementation decisions
- Identify long-term technical risks and mitigation approaches

### Goals
- Keep the solution aligned with architectural principles and scalability needs
- Reduce avoidable rework and technical debt
- Support sustainable delivery across the project lifecycle

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and design discussions
- Cross-team dependency and system integration planning
- Technical risk and roadmap alignment with leadership and engineering

---

## Sponsors / Executive Stakeholders

### Role Summary
Sponsors and executive stakeholders provide strategic direction, priority alignment, and escalation support for key initiatives. They help ensure the effort remains aligned to business outcomes and organizational commitments.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm business priority and funding for the initiative
- Align project goals with strategic objectives and stakeholder needs
- Support decision-making on major scope, resourcing, and escalation issues
- Help remove blockers that require leadership intervention
- Review progress against business value and key milestones

### Goals
- Ensure the project remains aligned with organizational priorities
- Improve executive awareness of risks, trade-offs, and outcomes
- Support timely decisions that enable successful delivery

### Typical Communication
- Steering updates, milestone reviews, and executive briefings
- Escalation for risks, dependencies, or strategic trade-offs
- Decisions on prioritization, funding, and strategic alignment

---

## Customer / End-User Advocates

### Role Summary
Customer or End-User Advocates represent the real-world needs and experience of the people who will use the product or service. They help ensure that delivery decisions stay grounded in usability and customer value.

### Responsibilities
- Represent user needs, pain points, and feedback
- Help define or validate acceptance criteria and usability expectations
- Provide input on prioritization, improvement opportunities, and adoption barriers
- Connect product decisions to real-world customer outcomes
- Support stakeholder understanding of user experience and expectations

### Goals
- Improve customer value and usability of the delivered solution
- Reduce the risk of building features that do not meet user needs
- Support product-market fit and adoption outcomes

### Typical Communication
- User research, feedback synthesis, and usability review sessions
- Product and acceptance criteria discussions
- Customer-impact updates during milestones and release planning

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These expanded role definitions clarify accountability, collaboration boundaries, and escalation paths across planning, execution, review, and release activities.
- They help teams implement clearer ownership models and improve cross-functional coordination.
